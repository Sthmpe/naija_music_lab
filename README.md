# Naija Music Lab

A private, evidence-led research repository for studying songwriting, vocal phrasing, language, rhythm and production in Nigerian and African popular music. It extracts reusable creative principles. Complete lyrics and commercial audio may be held as private working inputs under `AGENTS.md`; they are not public datasets or the final research product. The project does not redistribute recordings or provide artist-cloning recipes.

## Start here

Read `README.md`, `AGENTS.md`, `PROJECT_STATE.md`, `DECISIONS.md`, then the latest entries in `SESSION_LOG.md` and `CHANGELOG.md`. Check `CURRENT_ARTIST`, `CURRENT_STAGE`, `NEXT_ACTION`, and blockers before editing. `artists/` holds artist-specific research; `docs/` defines methods; `schemas/` supplies reusable records; `global/` holds cross-artist findings only after comparative evidence; `agents/` holds future specialist specifications.

## Progression

Work on one artist at a time. Reconnoitre projects and singles, present a project selection, and wait for user approval. Then inventory all tracks and present song recommendations; wait for song approval. Analyse each approved song independently before project and artist synthesis. Update state and append a session log after meaningful work. To resume an interrupted artist, read its `selection.md`, `source-index.md`, and any analyses alongside the state file; continue the recorded next action without repeating approved decisions.

## Adding artists

Add candidates to `ARTIST_ROADMAP.md`. Create a skeleton only when useful. Do not research or activate a new artist until the current artist is complete, reviewed and approved, then deliberately update `CURRENT_ARTIST` and log the decision.
