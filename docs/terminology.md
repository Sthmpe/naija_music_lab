# Terminology

**Project:** Distinct album, EP, mixtape or substantial collaborative release. **Expanded edition:** Deluxe/reissue of an existing project, catalogued but not counted as an independent original project. **Hook:** Recurrent memorable lyrical or melodic unit. **Cadence:** Timing of syllables, stresses and pauses against the musical pulse. **Negative space:** Audible gap or reduced instrumentation that frames a phrase. **Code-switching:** Movement between languages or registers. **Strong/repeated:** Recurs across substantial evidence. **Probable:** Several instances, incomplete sample. **Possible:** Limited evidence. **Project-specific/song-specific:** Restricted to that scope. **Verified/estimated/interpretation/unknown:** Provenance labels for observations.
