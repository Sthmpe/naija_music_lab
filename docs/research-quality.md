# Research quality

Each factual claim needs a traceable source. Measurements name the source audio, tool or listening method, and uncertainty. A claim such as “catchy” requires a mechanism: repetition, rhythmic cell, placement, vowels, melody or instrumental response. Distinguish what is heard from inferred artistic intent. Log contradictory release metadata rather than forcing agreement. Before synthesis, ask whether exceptions weaken a proposed pattern. Do not use chart or stream claims without a dated publisher source.
