# Source policy

Prefer artist/label pages and first-party streaming release pages for dates, credits and track order. Use music-industry chart publishers for chart claims. Record URL, access date, claim supported and any conflict. Lyrics may be consulted on reputable platforms and cross-checked for Nigerian Pidgin, Yoruba, Igbo or slang; do not assume one transcription is correct. Complete lyrics may be kept as private working inputs when reliably obtained, under the Raw Lyrics Working Layer in `AGENTS.md`; do not publish or export them as a public dataset. Keep only brief necessary excerpts in final analytical outputs. Audio must come from an authorized source or user-supplied file; do not redistribute it. Mark every field verified, measured, estimated, interpreted or unknown.
