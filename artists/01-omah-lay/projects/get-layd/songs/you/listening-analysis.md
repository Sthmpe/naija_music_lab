## Why Simple Lines Work

The clearest example is the extremely plain declaration built around:

**“I love … you.”**

On paper, almost nothing complicated is happening.

Its impact comes from performance architecture:

1. the opening words are rhythmically compact;
2. “love” is reiterated rather than replaced by new vocabulary;
3. **“you” receives the longest melodic release**;
4. the lead then leaves room for a response;
5. bass/percussion carry that gap;
6. the same cell returns before the listener's memory of the previous one has faded;
7. doubling/layering makes later occurrences feel larger without changing the core sentence.

The arrangement therefore turns lexical simplicity into repetition, anticipation and release.
