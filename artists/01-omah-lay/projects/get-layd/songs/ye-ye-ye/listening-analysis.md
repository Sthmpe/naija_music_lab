## Why Simple Lines Work

The simplest and strongest unit is just:

**`ye ye ye`**

Its effect depends on what surrounds it.

In the comparison passage the singer delivers relatively long, consonant-heavy clauses with repeated rhyme-like endings. When the hook returns, that density suddenly collapses to three small open vocables.

The mechanism is:

1. dense speech-shaped line;
2. stable bass/percussion underneath;
3. abrupt reduction to a tiny vocable;
4. three easy-to-recognise attacks;
5. brief opening;
6. faster backing answer;
7. reset and repeat.

The vocable becomes memorable **because of the contrast**, not because the syllable itself contains sophisticated lyrical information.
