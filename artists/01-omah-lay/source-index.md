## Metadata cautions

- Boy Alone displays July 14 or 15, 2022 depending on territory/platform. Use 2022-07-15 as the Spotify US page's date here; retain the one-day difference rather than treating it as a contradiction about track identity.
- One Apple Music deluxe page displays the original album's 2022 date even though Spotify and Warner identify the six-track expansion as 2023. Use Spotify's 2023-06-15 edition date.
- Streaming popularity order can change. No ranking or chart position is asserted from an undated streaming count.
- The One Call single page and some song-level displays differ on release date. Retain the single page date and resolve the conflict if the song is approved.
- You has a February 2020 Apple song date and May 2020 Get Layd/Shazam album date; determine whether the former denotes an earlier single/version.
- Shazam's understand page has a writer-credit inconsistency between its structured credit block and footer; woman has an unusual writer listing that needs publishing-source verification.
- Company is credited to Clean Bandit, Chlöe and Omah Lay according to Spotify and Apple. Its earlier solo label has been corrected to collaborative non-project/current release across current metadata and selection; the approved recording is unchanged and remains outside the six-case Feature Lab.
- Shazam's soso-with-Ozuna structured writer block omits Ozuna while the transcription includes Spanish passages and the footer differs from the block. Philo and People also have structured-credit/footer inconsistencies. Treat exact writing attribution as unresolved.
