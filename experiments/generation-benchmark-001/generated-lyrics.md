# One Ring

*Original Afro-fusion / Afropop lyric; intended feel around 104 BPM. Final revised version.*

## Intro

“I dey try, you know.”

[2-beat space]

That’s what I kept saying.

## Verse 1

Closed the shop in Yaba / ten past ten  
Keys left marks across my palm  
You texted, “Food’s on the stove.”  
No question at the end

I blamed traffic at Ojuelegba  
Like it held my tongue as well  
You used to ring me three times  
Now it’s one thumbs-up [pause]

## Hook

One ring, I pick  
One ring, I pick  
You won’t need to call me twice

[2-beat space]

One ring, I pick

## Verse 2

Tonight you called and asked, “Where do I fit  
Between the shop and all your plans?”  
I nearly said, “After December”  
The ledger open on my lap

[pause]

I closed it when you said,  
“You hear me, but you’re gone.”  
I had my speech about the rent  
I let it pass for once

## Hook — changed line

One ring, I pick  
One ring, I pick  
I should’ve heard you the first time

[2-beat space]

One ring, I pick

## Bridge

At one, the road goes quiet  
Your cup’s beside the sink  
I leave my phone face up this time  
And let the room go still

## Final hook / outro

One ring, I pick  
One ring, I pick  
If you still feel like calling

[2-beat space]

One ring… [instrument answers]

A bus goes past the window  
The screen stays dark
