# Generation benchmark 001 — One Ring

LAB_STATE_AT_GENERATION: Get Layd first-pass complete; later Omah Lay projects not yet analysed

Date: 2026-09-28 (Africa/Lagos)

## Fixed brief for future reruns

Write one **original** Nigerian Afro-fusion/Afropop song at an intended 102–106 BPM feel. A young Lagos worker moves around the city to build a life; his partner feels that work and nights outside have made him emotionally unavailable. Move from defensive confidence through admitted responsibility to restrained vulnerability, ending without a perfect resolution. Show the relationship through actions, small details and conversation. Use conversational English with natural Nigerian English/Pidgin only where it belongs. Give the hook a small, repeatable vocabulary and allow rests and arrangement answers. Do not imitate the researched artist or reuse any source lyric, melody, catchphrase, signature image or song-specific form.

Deliver a final lyric, short cadence notes, research-to-draft trace, and a five-perspective self-critique. Keep the benchmark separate from research findings and preserve this brief for later comparisons after more projects/artists have been studied.

## Benchmark status

This is a **generation test**, not an Omah Lay analysis or a new empirical songwriting rule. There is no recorded demo, scored audio evaluation, native-speaker panel or listener-memory test. Musical-fit and hook-memorability judgments are editorial predictions only. The approved Omah Lay corpus and current research stage stay unchanged.
